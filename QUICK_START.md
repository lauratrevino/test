## What Gets Tracked

- **Pageviews** — Every page load
- **Unique visitors** — Based on IP + browser
- **Events** — Button clicks (Donate, Volunteer, Merch, etc.)
- **Traffic sources** — Direct, Google, Facebook, etc.
- **Devices** — Mobile, Desktop, Tablet
- **Session duration** — How long people stay
- **Realtime visitors** — Who's online right now

## Privacy

✅ No cookies  
✅ No personal data  
✅ No third-party services  
✅ In-memory storage (resets on server restart)
